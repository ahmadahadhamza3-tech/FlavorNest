Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

At FlavorNest, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you visit our website.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal identifying information unless you voluntarily submit it (for example, when subscribing to our newsletter or using our contact form). Local settings such as your light/dark theme preference and bookmarked recipes are stored solely on your device via standard browser local storage.

Cookies & Third-Party Services

We may use standard web analytics and font providers (such as Google Fonts) to improve the reading and cooking experience. No sensitive personal data is shared or sold to third parties.

Contact

If you have any questions regarding our privacy practices, feel free to contact us through our Contact page.
